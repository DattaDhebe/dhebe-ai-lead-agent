Upload the contents of this folder into `public_html/api` on Hostinger.

Important:
- This folder intentionally excludes the real `.env` file.
- Keep your existing `public_html/api/.env` on the server, or recreate it there with your live credentials and API keys.
- This folder also excludes the old empty `api/` duplicate directory from the workspace backend.

Suggested steps:
1. Open Hostinger File Manager.
2. Go to `public_html/api`.
3. Upload or paste all files and folders from `backend-upload`.
4. Do not delete or replace the live `.env` unless you want to update its values.
